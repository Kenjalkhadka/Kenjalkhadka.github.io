About Me

Originally from Nepal, I am pursuing a degree in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Cincinnati. My interests include engine design, aerospace systems, propulsion, and mechanical component development.

I am a curious and determined learner who enjoys taking on new challenges and learning from experience. I am always open to new opportunities that allow me to grow, develop my skills, and gain meaningful engineering experience.

My Motivation

Why Mechanical Engineering?

My interest in Mechanical Engineering comes from my curiosity about automobiles, engines, and the mechanisms that make machines work. I have always been fascinated by how individual components come together to create complex systems and how engineering principles turn ideas into reality.

As I explored the field, my interests expanded beyond automobiles into aerospace systems, propulsion, and mechanical design. Through engineering projects and hands-on experiences, I have been able to explore these interests and develop a deeper understanding of the field.

For me, engineering is about curiosity, creativity, problem-solving, and continuously improving ideas.

"With passion comes creativity. With creativity comes innovation."
